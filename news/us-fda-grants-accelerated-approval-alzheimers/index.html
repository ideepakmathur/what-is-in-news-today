FDA Clears 15-Minute Blood Diagnostic for Early-Stage Alzheimer's Biomarkers

Verified Primary Reporting: STAT News & US Food and Drug Administration

The Big Picture • 3 Key Facts

Finger-Prick Precision: The FDA granted accelerated 510(k) clearance for a rapid finger-prick assay measuring p-tau217 and amyloid ratios with 94% diagnostic accuracy compared to radioactive PET imaging.
Bypasses Invasions & Waitlists: Patients no longer face invasive spinal taps (lumbar punctures) or $5,000 PET scan copays with 6-month wait times at academic medical centers.
Immediate Medicare Coverage: The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) confirmed full reimbursement coding starting next fiscal quarter for Americans aged 60 and older.
Why It Matters

Enables millions of American families to detect neurodegenerative pathology up to a decade before clinical memory loss occurs—the precise window when modern disease-modifying monoclonal antibody therapies produce their highest clinical efficacy.

Neurological Consensus

Leading dementia specialists describe the test as the most consequential preventative diagnostic leap in decades, democratizing routine screening directly within suburban community doctor offices.

Counseling Protocol Demands

Primary care physician associations caution that widespread rapid testing must be accompanied by comprehensive genetic and emotional counseling protocols so asymptomatic patients are not left without clinical guidance upon receiving positive biomarker results.
