Privacy & Cookie Policy

WhatIsInNewsToday.com ("we", "our", or "the Service") respects your privacy. We are committed to transparency in how information is collected, used, and protected. We do not sell user personal data to third-party data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

Email Newsletter Subscribers: When you subscribe to our Daily or Weekly Intelligence Digest, we collect only your email address and frequency preference. You may unsubscribe at any time using our one-click unsubscribe tool.

Server Telemetry & Cookies: We collect aggregate web analytics (browser type, referral headers, operating system) to optimize page loading speeds and cache distribution. We do not store invasive cross-site tracking cookies.

2. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is collected and processed.
  • Request complete deletion of stored email addresses.
  • Opt-out of any future commercial communication.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your constitutional and state privacy rights.
To submit a CCPA inquiry or deletion request, email: privacy@whatisinnewstoday.com.

3. Data Security & Retention

All newsletter recipient records are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 protocols and transported exclusively over secure HTTPS/TLS 1.3 channels. We do not maintain unencrypted local email logs.
