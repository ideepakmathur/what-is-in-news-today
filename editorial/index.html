Ethics & Verification Charter

Editorial Standards & Curation Methodology

At WhatIsInNewsToday.com, we believe Americans are inundated with information overload, sensationalist partisan clickbait, and algorithmic noise. Our mission is to deliver clean, verified, noise-free daily news intelligence with zero sensationalism.

1

The Smart Brevity Principle

Inspired by Axios and Semafor editorial philosophies, every news item published on our network conforms to a strict 3-part anatomy:

  • The Core Facts (3 Bullets): What actually occurred, stripped of rhetorical flourish, loaded framing, or speculation.
  • Why It Matters: Direct real-world consequences for American families, small business owners, workers, and taxpayers.
  • Multi-Perspective Balance: Where legitimate debate exists, we present both the consensus and the principal counterpoints side-by-side.
2

Primary Source Verification & Anti-Hallucination

We do not synthesize rumors or anonymous blog claims. Every story must trace directly to verified primary dispatches:

Institutional Sources

Federal Reserve Board, Supreme Court dockets, FDA regulatory releases, NASA flight records, DOT grant notices.

Tier-1 Wire Networks

The Associated Press, Reuters, Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, STAT News, and SCOTUSblog.

3

Top 10 Viral Trend Methodology

Our Top 10 rankings across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Google Search are computed algorithmically based on 24-hour play velocity, comment sentiment, and cross-platform reshare acceleration. We filter out inorganic bot spam and artificial engagement rings to surface authentic cultural moments.

4

Corrections & Editorial Inquiries

If an error of fact occurs, we issue transparent corrections at the top of the affected article within 60 minutes of notification.
